Takeaways

  • Zero-crash goal: the final model landed 997/1000 eval episodes (99.7%), with 3 failures — goal not reached within the step cap
  • Crash causes shifted: in the baseline eval the dominant cause was too-fast impact (563 of 565 failures); in the final eval it was too-fast impact (2 of 3 failures).
  • Hardest starts (final eval): start_dist in [1.10, 1.30] — 99% success (n=134); start_speed in [0.45, 0.60] — 98% success (n=170).
  • Fuel: mean fuel used went from 3.6 to 4.0 units per episode — the policy learned to brake harder (more fuel) when the speed limits started being enforced.
  • Why the old 68% number changed: the original env zeroed velocity before the landing check, so the |vy| ≤ 1.5 and |vx| ≤ 1.0 speed limits never applied and the agent learned to slam into the pad. The fix (judge the pre-contact impact velocity) dropped the same model to 42% on the same task — every later number here is measured with the fixed criteria.
